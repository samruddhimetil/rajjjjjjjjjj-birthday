We have had our sweetest moments and our difficult days too. There were times when things weren't easy, but every experience has become a part of our story. I hope we keep choosing each other, not just for birthdays, but for all the ordinary days and extraordinary moments waiting for us.

I want you to know how proud I am of you. I want to see you achieve every dream you have ever spoken about. I want to see you grow, succeed, become the person you dream of being and enjoy every little victory along the way. Even when life gets difficult, never doubt yourself. I will always wish the very best for you.

And my honey, I love you so, so hard. More than these words can ever explain. I can't wait to spend my whole life with you. I dream about all the little things too: celebrating birthdays together, annoying each other, travelling, laughing over absolutely nothing, supporting each other's dreams and growing old with a thousand stories to tell.

I don't expect every day to be perfect. I just hope that whatever life brings, we keep understanding each other, communicating, respecting each other and never stop making beautiful memories. I want a love that grows with time, becomes stronger with every chapter and feels like home.

On your birthday, I wish you endless happiness, good health, success, peace and all the love your heart deserves. Never stop smiling that smile of yours. And please never forget how special you are to me.

Thank you for being a part of my life, for the memories, for the laughter and for giving me so many reasons to believe in our little story.

Here's to this birthday, to everything we've been through, to everything we are today and to all the beautiful years I hope we get to share.

I love you endlessly, my Raj. Happy Birthday once again, my favourite boy. ♡
